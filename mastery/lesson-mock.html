Is √2 a fraction?

First the precise definitions, then what they mean in plain words.

Definition · rational number
A real number x is rational if x=ab for some a,b∈Z with b≠0. Otherwise x is irrational.
In plain words: a fraction, one whole number divided by another, like 7/5. (Z means the whole numbers, negatives included.)
Definition · even and odd
An integer n is even if n=2k for some k∈Z, and odd if n=2k+1 for some k∈Z.
In plain words: even means "2 times a whole number"; odd means "one more than that". 10 = 2 × 5 is even; 7 = 2 × 3 + 1 is odd.
Definition · lowest terms
ab is in lowest terms if gcd(a,b)=1.
In plain words: a and b share no common factor bigger than 1, so the fraction can't be simplified. 6/4 isn't; 3/2 is.
Theorem
2 is irrational.
Proof
  1. Pretend. Suppose not: 2=ab with a,b∈Z, b≠0, gcd(a,b)=1.
    We pretend √2 is a fraction already simplified as far as it goes, and watch what breaks.
  2. Remove the square root.
    2=a2b2⟹a2=2b2
    Square both sides: √2 squared is 2, and a/b squared is a²/b². Then multiply both sides by b².
  3. So a is even. a2=2(b2) is even, hence a is even.
    Why does a² even force a even?If a=2m+1 were odd, then a2=2(2m2+2m)+1, which is odd. In plain words: odd × odd is odd (3 × 3 = 9).
  4. Name the half. Write a=2k with k∈Z.
    That's just the definition of even. If a were 10, k would be 5.
  5. Put it back.
    (2k)2=2b2⟹b2=2k2
    4k² = 2b², then halve both sides. By the same reasoning as step 3, b is even too.
  6. Contradiction. 2∣a and 2∣b, so gcd(a,b)≥2, contradicting gcd(a,b)=1. ∎
    a and b both divide by 2, but step 1 said they share no common factor. Both can't be true, so the pretence was false.
The method
This is proof by contradiction: to prove a statement, assume it is false and derive something impossible.
